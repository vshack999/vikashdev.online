Cyber Crime Threat Intel

TOP 10 PRO TIPS & HIDDEN DEFENSE

1 / 10
METADATA EXPLOIT

Photo EXIF Se Exact GPS Leak

Camera se kheenchi gayi image me aapke latitude, longitude aur phone specs chupe hote hain. Publicly send karne par koi bhi bot aapka address plot kar leta hai.

🛡️ Solution: Camera settings me "Save Location" band rakhein ya share karne se pehle screenshot lein.
ANDROID ZERO-DAY VECTOR

Accessibility Overlay Trojan

Modded APK jab "Accessibility Service" allow karwata hai, to screen reading on ho jati hai. Aapka UPI PIN background me capture hota hai.

🛡️ Solution: Kisi bhi unverified modded APK ko Accessibility permission na dein.
ACCOUNT HIJACKING

Silent Cookie Theft (Bina OTP Hack)

Malware aapke browser ke `Session Cookies` chura kar bina OTP ke WhatsApp Web aur Instagram open kar lete hain.

🛡️ Solution: Monthly "Log out all sessions" karein aur unknown PC .exe files ko run na karein.
HARDWARE EXPLOIT

Public USB Charging (Juice Jacking)

Railway station ya airport par free USB charging port me hackers hidden chip laga dete hain. Cable lagate hi data transfer shuru ho jata hai.

🛡️ Solution: Hamesha apna Powerbank ya direct wall adapter use karein.
PAYMENT FRAUD

QR Code Hijacking (Quishing)

Public jagah (petrol pump/shops) par hackers real QR ke upar apna fake QR sticker chipka dete hain. Scan karte hi payment unke account me chali jati hai.

🛡️ Solution: Scan karne ke baad Merchant Name screen par dhyan se verify karein.
SOCIAL ENGINEERING

AI Voice Cloning & Deepfake

Sirf 3 second ke audio se hacker aapke family member ki aawaz clone karke phone par emergency bata kar paise maangte hain.

🛡️ Solution: Family ke sath ek "Secret Safe Word" banayein. Call par doubt ho to pooch lein.
NETWORK SNIFFING

Free Public Wi-Fi Traps

Hackers mall me "Free Wi-Fi" fake hotspot banate hain. Connect karte hi aapke passwords aur private activity unke paas chali jati hai (MitM Attack).

🛡️ Solution: Free Wi-Fi par banking app na kholein. Hamesha premium VPN use karein.
WIRELESS ATTACK

Bluetooth Sniffing (Bluebugging)

Public me Bluetooth ON chhodne se hackers 10 meter ki range se aapke device me ghus kar messages padh sakte hain aur data chura sakte hain.

🛡️ Solution: Earbuds use ke baad Bluetooth aur "Discoverable mode" hamesha OFF karein.
MALWARE INFECTION

Hidden Keyloggers & Fake Keyboards

Free fancy keyboard apps aapke type kiye gaye har ek letter (passwords, chats) ko record karke remote server par bhejte hain.

🛡️ Solution: Hamesha Gboard/Apple system keyboard use karein. Fancy fonts app avoid karein.
TELECOM FRAUD

SIM Card Cloning & Swap

Hacker aapki fake ID se telecom store ja kar naya SIM nikalwa lete hain. Purana SIM band hote hi banking OTP unhe aane lagte hain.

🛡️ Solution: Agar network achanak gayab ho jaye to turant operator aur bank ko call karein.
